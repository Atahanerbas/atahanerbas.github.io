# Logic and Philosophy

## Profile
My name is Atahan. I am a doctoral researcher with primary interests in formal logic, philosophical methodology, and diagrammatic reasoning. I also maintain a personal interest in the broader connections between logic and philosophy.

## Academic Appointment
I am currently a PhD candidate at the Institute of Philosophy, KU Leuven, under the supervision of Lorenz Demey and Stef Frijters. My doctoral project investigates the philosophy of logical geometry, with a particular emphasis on Aristotelian diagrams.
